FODS Viva Examination Quick Reference

When presenting to your course professor or viva evaluators:

  • Why separate chaining for Hash Tables? Handles arbitrary collisions cleanly via linked list buckets with O(1) average lookup.
  • Why Max-Heap for the Leaderboard? Extracting the top-K mentors runs in O(K log N) without needing full O(N log N) sorting of all students.
  • Why Trie for Skill Autocomplete? Prefix matching runs in O(L) proportional only to prefix character length, independent of total database size.
  • Why Adjacency List for Graphs? College peer networks are sparse; adjacency list uses O(V + E) space compared to O(V²) for an adjacency matrix.
